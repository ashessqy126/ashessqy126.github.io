I received my B.S. degree in Information Security from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC), and my M.S. degree in Computer Science and Technology from [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/). In 2021, I also worked as a research assistant at [George Mason University](https://www.gmu.edu/) (GMU) under the supervision of Prof. [Kun Sun](https://csis.gmu.edu/ksun/). Currently, I am a third-year Ph.D. student at the [Institute of Information Engineering](https://www.iie.ac.cn/) (IIE), Chinese Academy of Sciences.

My current research interests include:

* Software Security
* Web3 Security
* Intrusion Detection Systems

I welcome collaboration and am always open to discussing related academic topics. Feel free to contact me via [email](mailto:songqiyang@iie.ac.cn)—I’m eager to connect with researchers from around the world.
